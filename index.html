The Red Panda

Red pandas are small mammals with reddish-brown fur, ringed tails, and expressive faces. They spend much of their time in trees in the mountain forests of the eastern Himalayas and southwestern China. Although they are related to neither giant pandas nor raccoons, red pandas are excellent climbers and use their long tails for balance.

Red Panda Facts

  • Habitat: Cool, high-altitude temperate forests with bamboo.
  • Diet: Mostly bamboo, along with fruit, insects, and small animals.
  • Size: About the size of a large house cat, with a long, fluffy tail.
